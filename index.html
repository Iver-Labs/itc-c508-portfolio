Course Expectation // Question A

Deep Learning & Predictive Transparency

In this BSIT course, students gain a comprehensive understanding of core information technology principles. The curriculum focuses on developing technical proficiency, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills necessary for the modern IT landscape. Students engage with complex neural architectures, database management, and strategies for addressing the "black box" challenge to improve model transparency, interpretability, and trustworthiness.

The course aims to bridge the gap between theoretical mathematical models and practical execution. Expect a rigorous academic environment where collaboration and independent research are highly valued. By the end of this program, students are proficient in implementing scalable IT solutions with TensorFlow, Keras, and PyTorch while maintaining strict ethical standards and security best practices.
