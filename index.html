Course Expectations

A couple of reflection prompts on what this course expects from its students, and how deep learning connects to real business problems.

Question A

Identify and describe a specific business problem that can be addressed using deep learning-based predictive models. First, explain the nature and impact of the business problem. Then, discuss how deep learning can be applied to solve it, including strategies for addressing the “black box” challenge to improve the transparency, interpretability, and trustworthiness of the model’s predictions.

In this BSIT course, students are expected to gain a comprehensive understanding of core information technology principles. The curriculum focuses on developing technical proficiency, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills necessary for the modern IT landscape. Students will engage with complex systems, networking fundamentals, and database management, preparing them for real-world applications and professional environments.

The course aims to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical execution. Expect a rigorous academic environment where collaboration and independent research are highly valued. By the end of this program, students should be proficient in navigating emerging technologies and implementing scalable IT solutions while maintaining ethical standards and security best practices.
